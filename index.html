Help & Tutorial

Welcome to MediRemind!

This application helps you stay on track with your medication schedule.

How to Use:

  • Go to the **Home** screen and tap the **+** button to add a new medication reminder.
  • Use the toggles next to each medicine card on the Home screen to mark it as **Taken**. This updates your adherence score!
  • Check your **Profile** to see your monthly progress and adherence rate.
  • Use the **Calendar** to see which medications are scheduled for a specific day.
  • Use the **Trash Can Icon** next to any medicine card to permanently **delete** a reminder.

Data Persistence:

All reminders and profile edits are saved locally on your browser. Clearing your browser data will reset the app.
